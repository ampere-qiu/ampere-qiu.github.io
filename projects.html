BricksTory

A playful child–AI co-creation system that brings physical brick building and interactive storytelling together.

In the project introduction, children build a scene with bricks, share it with the system through a photo and optional text or speech, then work with an AI storytelling partner. The system interprets the scene, extends the story, and helps keep its details consistent while leaving the child’s ideas at the centre.

This project connects my personal interest in LEGO building with research into AI-supported creative play. Our paper, Thinking by Hand: Storybook Creation from Brick Building with AI Support, was presented as an interactive demo at the Entertainment Computing Symposium in 2025.
