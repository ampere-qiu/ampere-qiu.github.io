A little about me

I am a Lecturer at the Faculty of Information Science and Engineering, Ritsumeikan University, Japan. My work brings together human–computer interaction, human–AI collaboration, learning, communication, and creative play.

I’m also an AFOL (Adult Fan of LEGO), a LEGO MASTERS JAPAN 2023 contestant, and an organizing member of Japan Brickfest. This hands-on hobby also inspires BricksTory, my research into AI-supported storytelling through brick building. Visit Lovely Bricks to see my LEGO projects.

Email: mond-p@fc.ritsumei.ac.jp
