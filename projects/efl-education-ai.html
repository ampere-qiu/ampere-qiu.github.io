Research · Learning & education

EFL Education with AI

Helping English learners practise speaking through playful, AI-supported activities.

All projects

The question

How can AI partners make speaking practice approachable while giving learners useful opportunities to express ideas in English?

Taboo Talks

Taboo Talks is a browser-based word-guessing game for English learners. A player describes a target word without using related taboo words while a partner guesses. The project explores human and AI partners, using speech recognition and language-model responses to make the activity conversational.

What the study found

A study comparing AI and human partners reported improvements in some measures of speaking fluency and complexity after practice with the AI partner, while accuracy remained broadly stable. Learners described the AI partner as less intimidating, while also noting limits in its responsiveness. These exploratory findings help guide future activity design.

Selected work

Pituxcoosuvarn, M., Tanimura, M., Murakami, Y., & White, J. S. “Enhancing EFL Speaking Skills with AI-Powered Word Guessing: A Comparison of Human and AI Partners.” Information, 16(6), 427, 2025.
