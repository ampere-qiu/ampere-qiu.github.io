Research · Learning & education

EFL Education with AI

Making it easier for English learners to practise speaking, vocabulary, and self-expression with playful AI activities.

All projects

A simple idea

Speaking a new language takes practice. Games can make that practice feel less like a test and more like a conversation. This work explores how AI can play a helpful partner while learners describe ideas in English.

How Taboo Talks works

A learner sees a secret word and explains it without saying the word or a few related “taboo” words. A partner listens and guesses. The system combines speech recognition and a language model to make this familiar word game interactive.

What the comparison suggests

A 2025 study compared learners practising with an AI partner and with a human partner. Some measures of fluency and complexity improved after AI-partner practice, while accuracy stayed broadly similar. Learners found the AI partner less intimidating, but also wanted it to respond more naturally. The findings are early evidence, not a claim that AI replaces a teacher or conversation partner.

Projects within this theme

Taboo Talks · speaking through a word game

Learners describe words and practise speaking aloud. The studies look at how the game works and how learners respond to AI and human partners.

Heritage-language flashcards · a related learning design

A separate design proposal explores using an LLM to create flashcards for people beginning to learn a family or community language. It extends the theme of making language practice more personal and accessible.
