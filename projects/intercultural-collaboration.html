Research · Multilingual communication

AI for Intercultural Collaboration

Designing communication support that helps people notice and navigate differences across languages and cultures.

All projects

The question

Words, expressions, and visual cues can carry different associations across languages and communities. How can communication tools help people understand these differences without making assumptions for them?

Research directions

This research includes machine-translation-mediated communication, cultural-misunderstanding warnings, confidence cues, and analysis of language and emoji. The goal is to help collaborators notice when a phrase may be interpreted differently and decide what clarification would help.

A design principle

Support should make uncertainty visible and invite mutual understanding. A warning about a possible cultural mismatch needs to communicate how confident the system is, so people can judge whether to ask, explain, or continue the conversation.

Selected work

Selected work includes “Confidence Value for Cultural-Based Misunderstanding Warning in Multilingual Chat” (HCII 2022), “Effect of Cultural Misunderstanding Warning in MT-Mediated Communication” (CollabTech 2020), and recent analyses of Japanese–English emoji use.
