Research · Multilingual communication

AI for Intercultural Collaboration

Building communication tools that help people understand one another across language and cultural differences.

All projects

The problem

The same word, image, or emoji can bring different associations to different people. Translation tools can help people communicate, but they may not show when meaning or tone could be misunderstood.

A few ways into the problem

This work studies cultural difference detection, warnings about possible misunderstandings, and cues that show how confident the system is. Newer projects also examine word impressions and Japanese–English emoji use.

The goal

The tools are meant to help people notice where their interpretations may differ and decide whether to explain or ask a question. They support understanding; they do not decide what a culture or a person means.

Projects within this theme

Misunderstanding warnings

Earlier studies explored when a system should flag a possible cultural mismatch and how a confidence value could help people judge that warning.

Finding differences in words and images

Projects examine whether image features or word associations reveal differences that may matter in multilingual interaction.

Emoji across languages

Recent work compares emoji use in Japanese and English. Emoji are small visual cues, but their use and associations can differ between language communities.
