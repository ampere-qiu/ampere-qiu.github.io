Research · Human–AI collaboration

Facilitator Agent

Exploring how AI can help people keep up with multilingual group discussions and take part more fully.

All projects

Why a facilitator?

In a group using machine translation, some people may have a harder time following the conversation—especially when their language has fewer translation resources. An AI facilitator could help by making the discussion easier to understand and follow.

What the agent can do

The research explores tools that summarize what has been said and help participants keep track of the discussion. Other studies look at facilitation in children’s multilingual activities, where the system needs to support the group without taking over.

What we know so far

A 2024 study developed and examined two facilitator agents. The quantitative results did not show a clear overall effect. Interviews and observations suggested the agents may help low-resource-language speakers participate more fully. This points to a promising direction that needs further evaluation.

Projects within this theme

Multilingual discussion support

Summaries and facilitator agents aim to help participants understand the conversation, especially speakers of low-resource languages.

Children’s intercultural collaboration

A related research strand studies the facilitator’s role in children’s multilingual activities and the kinds of conversational moves that help a group work together.
