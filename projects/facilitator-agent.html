Research · Human–AI collaboration

Facilitator Agent

Exploring how an AI facilitator can help people follow and take part in multilingual group discussions.

All projects

The challenge

When people communicate through machine translation, participants who speak a low-resource language may find it harder to keep up with a fast-moving discussion. This can affect how fully they are able to participate.

The approach

This work investigates facilitator agents that help make group discussion easier to follow. The agents focus on summarizing conversation and supporting shared understanding, especially for participants who may otherwise be left behind.

What we learned

In the 2024 study, two facilitator agents were developed and validated. Quantitative analysis did not show a clear effect; qualitative analysis suggested the agents may help support fuller participation. Facilitation is a design opportunity that needs careful evaluation.

Selected work

Motozawa, M., Murakami, Y., Pituxcoosuvarn, M., Pariyar, A., & Aramaki, H. “Building a Facilitator Agent to Promote Understanding in Multilingual Communication.” InCIT 2024, pp. 804–809. Related work includes studies of facilitator agents in machine-translation-mediated communication.
