FROM HOBBY TO RESEARCH

BUILD IT.

THE STORY.

LEGO is part of my life as an adult fan and builder. It also connects with a question in my research: how can interactive technology support imagination and creative play?

BricksTory, developed with Yohei Murakami, brings hands-on brick building together with AI-generated storytelling. It explores ways to help children discover ideas and reinterpret what they have made.

THE BUILDER BEHIND THE BRICKS

Hi, I’m Ampere.
Builder, storyteller, researcher.

I’m Mondheera Pituxcoosuvarn — Ampere to friends, and @lovely_bricks on Instagram. I’m an adult fan of LEGO and a LEGO MASTERS JAPAN 2023 contestant. I’m also part of the Japan Brickfest organizing team, where I was Editor-in-Chief of the 2026 event magazine.

I’m also a lecturer and human–computer interaction researcher at Ritsumeikan University in Japan. My interests in hands-on making and storytelling meet my work on learning, creative play, and human–AI collaboration. Lovely Bricks brings those worlds together.
